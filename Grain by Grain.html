Tibetan monks make dul-tson-kyil-khor, mandalas of coloured powder, by tapping sand through a thin metal funnel called a chak-pur. The work can take days. When it is finished it is swept to the centre and the sand is poured into moving water.

Every mandala here is drawn from a new random seed and exists only once. You can sweep it at any point. Nothing is saved.
